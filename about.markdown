![Headshot](/headshot.jpeg)

I am currently a Software Engineer at a [satellite communications startup](https://rbcsignals.com/).

I became interested in satellite communications working as an undergraduate research assistant under the mentorship of Dr. Whitney Lohmeyer. During this time I published [four papers](https://scholar.google.com/citations?hl=en&view_op=list_works&gmla=AETOMgFPr1zKNTvL9A4lsXvrEJDX3GDx7lzzr6Rl2tWL_7zdBoccZ5eNUtP0FUU38SHsiYLWqXQFFUjVTEo3e77UYlBN&user=UhDMLdgAAAAJ) on various topics in satellite communications and space policy. I also led the ground station team for the SWARM-EX cubesat mission.

I studied applied math in undergrad. Some of my favorite classes were Abstract Algebra, Number Theory, and Real Analysis. During undergrad I built the following math-related projects: [satellite image compression using the SVD and convolutional autoencoders](https://github.com/zaynpatel/MatrixMethods), [hamming (7,4) error correction codes (ecc) on hardware](https://github.com/olincollege/HammingCodeHardware), and [text analysis using latent semantic analysis](https://github.com/zaynpatel/LTA2061). I also gave talks on [linear regression from scratch](https://github.com/zaynpatel/TAAS/tree/main/Deliverable_1) and [matrix calculus](https://github.com/zaynpatel/TAAS/tree/main/Deliverable_3). Since I graduated I have given talks on [Cayley digraphs of groups](https://github.com/zaynpatel/cayley-digraphs-groups) and [self-studied elementary probability theory](https://github.com/zaynpatel/stat110).

I also like to run. I ran 767.4 miles (1235 km) in 2025.
